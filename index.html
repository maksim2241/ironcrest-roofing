FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us.

Still unsure? Talk to a project manager at (713) 555-0148.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Houston?

It depends on roof size, pitch, material and how many layers need to come off. After a free inspection you get a written, line-item estimate — so you know the exact price before any work starts.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover storm damage?

Many policies cover sudden damage from hail and wind. We document the damage with photos, provide a detailed report and can meet your adjuster on site. Coverage decisions are always made by your insurance company.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes are completed in one day, two at most for larger or steeper roofs. Weather can shift the schedule, and we'll keep you updated.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Every installation includes a written workmanship warranty, in addition to the manufacturer's material warranty. We walk you through both at the final inspection.

Do I need to be home during the work?

Not necessarily. We just need access to the driveway and an exterior outlet. We'll message you photos as work progresses and at completion.

What should I do if my roof is leaking right now?

Move valuables away from the leak, place a bucket under drips, and call our emergency line at (713) 555-0148. Don't climb onto a wet roof yourself.
